The idea

Autonomous doesn't mean unattended.

The crew does the work of a whole production team. You keep the decisions that cost money or can't be taken back. That split is the system, and it's what this course teaches.

The crew does

  • Builds the apps, games and pages, then tests them
  • Makes the videos: script, voices, clips, edit, score
  • Ships the uploads, the deploys and the emails you approve
  • Writes down every correction, so tomorrow's session already knows

You keep the keys

  • The money: every paid button, budget and daily cap
  • The go-live: nothing deploys until you say so
  • The passwords: you type them, the crew never does
  • The final cut: your eye and ear beat any meter

Questions

Straight answers.

Do I need to know how to code?

You don't write the code. You do need to say clearly what you want, read what comes back, and make the calls. Every chapter shows the exact words we used, so you can see what clear looks like.

What tools does it use?

Claude Code runs the crew, and Lesson 1 walks you through installing it. It needs a paid Claude plan (Pro or higher). The paid parts of our studio (AI video, images and voices) are pay-as-you-go, and the course shows where the money goes and how to cap it before you spend a dollar.

We don't promise income. We teach the system we run every day; what you build with it, and what that earns, is up to you.
